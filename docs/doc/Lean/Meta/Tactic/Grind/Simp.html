      def Lean.Meta.Grind.symNorm (e : Expr) (methods : Sym.Simp.Methods) (dmethods : Sym.DSimp.Methods) (s : Sym.Simp.State) (ds : Sym.DSimp.State) :
      Sym.SymM (Simp.Result × Sym.Simp.State × Sym.DSimp.State)

      The Sym.simp-based normalization step: Sym.simp with methods, then Sym.dsimp of the result with dmethods. The two passes are independent, and their states are threaded through.

      Sym.simp does not visit the fixed arguments of an application, e.g. the implicit objects of a morphism composition, while the legacy normalizer dsimps them (Simp.Config.dsimp). A beta-redex or a projection of a structure instance left in such an argument makes the term miss the E-matching patterns of its theorems, so the dsimp pass reduces them. Its result is definitionally equal to the simp result, so the proof is the one of the simp pass.

      Sym requires kernel projections to be folded into projection applications. The legacy normalizer accepts them, so preprocessImpl folds them only afterwards; they are folded here first.
