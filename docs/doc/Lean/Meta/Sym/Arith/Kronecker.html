    def Lean.Meta.Sym.Arith.kronSatBits :
    Nat

    The estimates below saturate at 2 ^ kronSatBits, which is larger than the bit limit of every Kronecker certificate: an estimate that reaches it rejects the certificate (kronExponent?), and the estimate is never computed in full. Without saturation, ((x - x) ^ 64) ^ 64 … builds integers of billions of bits.

    Equations
    • Lean.Meta.Sym.Arith.kronSatBits = 2 ^ 22
