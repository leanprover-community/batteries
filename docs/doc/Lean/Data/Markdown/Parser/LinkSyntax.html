    The link destination and link title grammar of CommonMark §6.3. Shared by inline links and link reference definitions.

    def Lean.Markdown.parseLinkDestination? {s : String.Slice} (cur : InlineCursor s) :
    Option (LinkDestination s × InlineCursor s)

    Parses a link destination at cur. Returns the destination and the cursor after it.

    • A bracketed destination is <, then characters other than an unescaped < or > or a line ending, then >.
    • An unbracketed destination is a sequence of characters other than ASCII whitespace and control characters in which parentheses balance and nest at most 32 deep, and it ends before a ) that has no matching (. An unbracketed destination may be empty.

      def Lean.Markdown.parseLinkTitle? {s : String.Slice} (cur : InlineCursor s) :
      Option (LinkTitle s × InlineCursor s)

      Parses a link title at cur, which must point at its opener: ", ', or (. The content extends to the first unescaped closer that matches the opener: ", ', or ). An unescaped ( inside a parenthesized title fails. Returns the title and the cursor after its closer.
