    Projections of lattice operations #

    The lattice operations on functions, pairs and PProd are pointwise and componentwise: applying a function or projecting a component commutes with ⊤, ⊥, ⊓, ⊔, ⨅, ⨆, ⌜·⌝ and ⇨, and ⊑ on functions and pairs is pointwise and componentwise.
