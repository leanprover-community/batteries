    Complete lattice algebra #

    The laws of ⊤, ⊥, ⊓, ⊔, ⨅ and ⨆ at an abstract carrier: the order laws, the units, the monoid laws of ⊓ and ⊔, monotonicity, and the characterizations of ⊑ against the connectives.

    Derived laws of CompleteLattice #

    Lattice algebra derived from the laws of CompleteLattice: monotonicity of the connectives, the monoid laws of ⊓ and ⊔ with their units ⊤ and ⊥, and the characterizations of ⊑ against ⊤, ⊥, ⊓, ⊔, ⨅ and ⨆.
