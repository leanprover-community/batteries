    Polynomials with packed monomials, for kernel-checked normalization in commutative rings.

    The monomial x₀^k₀ * ... * xₙ^kₙ is packed as the natural number k₀ + k₁ * B + ... + kₙ * B^n for a base B larger than every degree, so multiplying monomials adds their codes and comparing them compares numbers, operations the kernel performs on literals directly. Each term also stores its degree, and terms are sorted by decreasing degree and then by increasing code, which is the order Mon.grevlex of Poly.
